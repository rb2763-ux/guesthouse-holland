Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This page explains what personal data is processed when you visit guesthouseholland.com, use the chat assistant or contact us, why this happens, and what rights you have. We keep it short and describe what the website actually does.

1. Who is responsible

For any privacy question or request, simply write to the email address above.

2. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) and delivered through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). When you open a page, these providers technically process your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request, in order to deliver the website and protect it against attacks. We do not use this data to identify you.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful. Google Analytics is only loaded after you click Accept in the cookie banner. It then sets cookies and processes usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, and a shortened IP address. Data may be transferred to the USA. Legal basis: your consent.

If you click Reject or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time: .

4. Chat assistant

On several pages you can chat with our booking assistant. It is operated for us by ChatPro AI (FITCOACHAI LTD, United Kingdom) and uses AI language models to answer your questions.

  • What is processed: the messages you type, and any details you choose to share, such as your name, email, phone number or travel dates.
  • Why: to answer your questions and handle your booking request.
  • Where: conversations are stored on ChatPro AI servers in Germany (EU). To generate answers, messages are sent to AI model providers, which may process them outside the EU.
  • Who sees it: Dre receives each conversation by email so he can reply to you personally.

Please do not enter sensitive information such as passport or payment card numbers in the chat. You can ask us at any time to delete your conversation.

5. Contact form, email and WhatsApp

The contact form does not send data to our server. It opens your own email program with your message addressed to us. When you email us or message us on WhatsApp (WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms), we use your details only to answer you and to handle your stay.

6. Content from other providers

To display this website, your browser loads content directly from these providers, which receive your IP address in the process:

  • Google Fonts (fonts), Google LLC
  • Unsplash (photos), Unsplash Inc.
  • YouTube (video preview images), Google LLC
  • flagcdn.com (flag icons for the language selector)
  • chatproai.io (the chat assistant)

Links to Booking.com, Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp only load those services when you click them.

7. Storage in your browser

Besides the Google Analytics cookies (only after consent), the website stores two small entries in your browser: your cookie choice and your chosen language. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

8. How long we keep data

Emails, chat conversations and booking details are kept as long as needed to handle your request and your stay, and as required by law. Google Analytics data is kept for the period set in Google Analytics, at most 14 months.

9. Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, and have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can also object to its use or request a copy. If you are in the European Union, you also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Just write to drebroeders@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If the website changes, we will update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
