9.2 on Booking.com: What Our Guests Actually Say

By Dre · November 18, 2026 · 5 min read

Dre — 🌿 ECO-Tourguide #001

I don't write about my own apartments very often. But when guests take the time to write something honest after their stay, I think it deserves to be read by the next person who is deciding where to stay in Juan Dolio.

Our guest rating on Booking.com now stands at 9.2 out of 10. That number is not something I can buy or polish. It is the average of what real guests wrote after they packed their bags and went home. So instead of telling you myself why you should stay with us, I'd rather let the people who already did speak for themselves.

The Scores, Category by Category

Booking.com asks every guest to rate six things. Here is where we stand today:

  • Staff: 9.8
  • Location: 9.7
  • Value for money: 9.7
  • Cleanliness: 9.2
  • Facilities: 8.9
  • Comfort: 8.9

Staff at the top makes me proud, because that is my wife and me. Location and value are the reasons most guests choose Juan Dolio in the first place: the beach is a short walk away, and you don't pay resort prices. Facilities and comfort are a little lower. They are still good scores, and they are exactly where I want guests to tell me what could be better. If something in your apartment is not right during your stay, tell me. I'd rather fix it on day two than read about it afterwards.

Hans from Sweden

Hans wrote his review on November 1. He rated staff, cleanliness, location, comfort and value with a 10:

"Nice and very functional accommodation in a great location and at a good price! The owner and his wife are very friendly, kind and genuinely care about your well-being, to make sure your stay is as pleasant as possible. And you live very close to the beach, it's just a short walk. To probably the most beautiful beach in all of Juan Dolio! I explored half of it, all the way to Ocean Tower at AquaMarine, the most beautiful I've seen here so far. Also close to the luxury hotel Coral Costa Caribe Beach!" — Hans, Sweden (translated from Swedish)

He is right about the beach. Many guests only walk the first few hundred meters, but it is a long beach, and walking it early in the morning, before it gets hot, is worth it.

J Robert from the USA

J Robert stayed with us for two weeks, wrote his review on September 8 and gave us a 10 in every category:

"Had a great two week stay. Comfortable. Had everything I needed including a cleaning lady who came during the stay. […] The property is super clean. It's situated right along the beach. 3-5 minute walk. Grocery stores, colmados, food kiosks and lots of restaurants and bars nearby. It's in a gated area and has easy access for those with keys at any hour. Safe area and the apartment has a great kitchen, TV, sofa bed, black out curtains, A/C, strong WiFi, and large bathroom with hot water. There was nothing negative about this property." — J Robert, USA

That list is a good summary of what a longer stay needs: a real kitchen, a quiet night's sleep and a supermarket close by. Two weeks is a stay where small things start to matter, and those are the things he mentions.

💡 Dre's Tip: Reading reviews before you book? Look at what guests say about the things that matter for your trip. Families ask about the beach and the kitchen, longer stays about WiFi and the supermarkets. If your question is not answered in any review, just ask me before you book.

Dre's Honest Take

A good score is nice, but it is not the reason I do this. After thirty years here, what I enjoy most is guests who leave knowing a bit more about the Dominican Republic than the hotel strip shows them. If the reviews tell me anything, it is that the simple things count: a clean apartment, a short walk to the sea, and someone who answers when you have a question. That is what we will keep doing.
